When a plot changes hands in Uttar Pradesh, two amounts go to the state before the deed is signed: stamp duty on the transfer and a registration fee for recording it. Together they are the largest cost after the land itself, and they are computed on a value the buyer often gets wrong.

## The value they are charged on

Both charges are a percentage of the same base: the higher of the circle value (area × the district's circle rate for that locality and land type) and the price written in the sale deed. The [circle rate guide](/guides/circle-rate-vs-market-rate-in-up/) explains where that rate comes from. In practice, on main roads the deed price is higher and sets the base; in interior villages the circle value often does.

## Stamp duty

The general rate in UP is 7% of the value. Women buyers pay a lower rate, 6%, through a 1% rebate that applies only up to a property-value cap; above the cap the general rate applies to the whole value. Joint purchases by a man and a woman are treated on their own footing. The rates the site's calculator applies, with their effective dates and the source notification, are shown on every circle-rate page.

<Callout title="Confirm before you budget" tone="warn">
Rates, the rebate and its cap change by notification. The figures above are the general position as this guide was written and carry a review note in the data. Check the circle-rate page for the effective date, or ask us for the current notification.

## Registration fee

The registration fee is 1% of the same value, paid at the sub-registrar's office when the deed is presented. Earlier schedules capped the fee; check the current notification for whether a cap applies. The fee is the same for every buyer category.

## Worked examples

A 1,200 sq ft residential plot on Faizabad Road, circle rate ₹12,000 per sq m, general rate:

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Area in the published unit | 111.48 sq m |
| Circle value | ₹13,37,804 |
| Stamp duty at 7% | ₹93,646 |
| Registration fee at 1% | ₹13,378 |
| Paid to the government | ₹1,07,024 |

The same plot bought in a woman's name at 6%, if the value is within the rebate cap, saves ₹13,378 of stamp duty. If the agreed price is ₹20 lakh instead of the circle value, every line is computed on ₹20 lakh. The [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator/) does this for any locality on the site and lets you switch the buyer category.

## What is not in these numbers

- **Brokerage.** Agreed separately, in writing, before any site visit. Never a percentage of the deed value you did not agree to.
- **Deed writing and legal fees.** The deed writer or lawyer's charge for drafting and attending registration.
- **Mutation.** Recording the transfer in the revenue record after registration carries a small fee at the tehsil and is a separate application.
- **Conversion.** If the plot is agricultural in the record, the Section 80 declaration is its own process and cost; see the [conversion guide](/guides/agricultural-to-residential-conversion-up/).

## Paying and proving it

UP uses e-stamping. The stamp certificate is generated for the computed value, the registration fee is paid against the same value, and both receipts go into the file with the deed. Keep them with the certified copy of the deed and the mutation order; the next buyer, or a bank, will ask for the set.
