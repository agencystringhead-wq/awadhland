## What an NRI can buy

Residential plots, houses and commercial land are open to NRIs. Agricultural land is not, and a lot of land sold around Ayodhya as a "plot" is still recorded as agricultural in the khatauni.

<Callout title="The one check that decides everything" tone="warn">
Before the price, before the location, read the land category in the khatauni. If it says agricultural, an NRI cannot buy it, whatever the seller calls it.
</Callout>

## Check the land before you pay

Pull the khatauni, confirm there is no encumbrance, check that mutation is complete, and match the plot against the master-plan land use.

<Checklist title="Documents to see before the token">
- Current khatauni for the exact khasra number, with the seller's name on it
- 13-year encumbrance search from the sub-registrar's office
- Mutation order for the last sale
- Master-plan land-use extract showing residential or commercial
- UP RERA number if the plot is inside a plotted development
</Checklist>

## Buying from abroad

Payment has to come through normal banking channels from an NRE or NRO account. If you cannot travel for registration, a properly executed power of attorney lets someone sign on your behalf.
