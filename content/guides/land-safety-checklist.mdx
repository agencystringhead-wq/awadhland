Most land disputes in UP start with something a buyer could have checked before paying the token: a khasra number that does not match the plot, a co-owner who never signed, Gram Sabha land sold as private, or a power-of-attorney "sale" that never transferred ownership. This checklist puts those checks in order.

It has 30 checks in five stages: identifying the land, the legal checks, the site visit, the money and the registry, and what to do after it. Tick a box only after you have seen the document yourself, not after someone tells you it is fine. One unticked box in stage 1 or 2 means don't pay the token yet.
