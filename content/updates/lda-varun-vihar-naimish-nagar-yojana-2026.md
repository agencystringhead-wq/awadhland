## LDA's new plot schemes at a glance

If you've been waiting for an LDA plot scheme in Lucknow, this is a big one. LDA opened online registration for **Varun Vihar Yojana** and **Naimish Nagar Yojana** together on 24 September 2026, and both close on **24 October 2026**.

LDA plots are popular for a simple reason. You're buying from a government development authority, the layout is planned, and the title comes from the authority itself. That's a lot of peace of mind compared with buying an unplanned plot from an individual seller.

But "government scheme" doesn't mean "no homework". More on that below.

## Varun Vihar Yojana: location, size and plots

**Varun Vihar Yojana (वरुण विहार योजना)** is LDA's large plotted township near the **Agra Expressway** in Lucknow.

- **Total planned area:** about **6,580 acres**, making it one of LDA's largest township schemes.
- **Phase 1:** **2,847 residential plots**, spread across **Kashi Khand** and **Kailash Khand**.
- **Plot sizes:** reported from **112.5 sq m up to 450 sq m**.
- **Reported rate:** around **₹2,800 per sq ft**.

Varun Vihar suits buyers who want a planned plot on Lucknow's western growth side with expressway access, and who are comfortable with a longer development timeline. The township is still being built out and parts of the land are still in acquisition. LDA's own booklet marks the images as indicative, so don't treat the brochure pictures as what you'll see on day one.

## Naimish Nagar Yojana: location and plots

**Naimish Nagar Yojana (नैमिष नगर योजना)** is on the **Sitapur Road** side of Lucknow, in the **Bakshi Ka Talab** area.

- **Plot sizes:** reported from about **72 sq m up to 450 sq m**.
- **Reported rate:** around **₹3,800 per sq ft**.
- **Registration:** same window as Varun Vihar, 24 September to 24 October 2026.

The smaller plot sizes make Naimish Nagar interesting for buyers with a tighter budget who still want an LDA plot. The higher per sq ft rate reflects its location on the Sitapur Road corridor.

Plot counts for Naimish Nagar vary between reports, so check the exact number of plots in each category in the official booklet.

## How to apply for LDA plots (step by step)

1. Go to the official portal: **registration.ldalucknow.in**.
2. Create an account or log in with your mobile number and email.
3. Find the scheme under online lotteries and select **Varun Vihar Yojana** or **Naimish Nagar Yojana**.
4. **Download and read the scheme booklet** before choosing a plot category.
5. Fill in your details and choose your plot size category.
6. Upload the required documents (identity, address and anything else the booklet asks for).
7. Pay the **₹1,100 registration fee** online.
8. Save the acknowledgement and payment receipt.

Do it yourself on the official portal. Don't hand your login or payment to an agent, and don't pay anyone who says they'll "help with selection".

## Lottery, allotment and payment

- **Allotment is by lottery**, done after registration closes. Nobody can guarantee you a plot.
- **Lottery date:** as of early October 2026, LDA hadn't announced it. Keep an eye on ldalucknow.in.
- **Instalments:** some reports mention an option of **36 instalments at around 9.7% interest**. Check the exact payment plan, interest and penalty terms in the booklet.
- **Refunds:** read the refund rules for unsuccessful applicants and for anyone who surrenders a plot after allotment. These differ by scheme.

## Why these areas of Lucknow are growing

Lucknow's growth has been pushing outward for years, and both schemes sit on growth corridors.

The **Agra Expressway** side, where Varun Vihar is, already benefits from strong road connectivity. And the **Awadh Expressway** (the Lucknow to Kanpur expressway), inaugurated on **13 July 2026**, has further improved the city's links on that side of the region.

The **Sitapur Road** and **Bakshi Ka Talab** belt, home to Naimish Nagar, has been growing as Lucknow expands north.

LDA has also been active elsewhere. **Anant Nagar Yojana** on Mohan Road ran its Phase 5 plot registration in August and September, and the **Aditi Apartment** (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana) in Anant Nagar has registration open till 2 November 2026.

## What to check before you apply

LDA plots are safer than most, but still read the fine print:

1. **Booklet first:** plot categories, exact rates, reservation quotas, eligibility and documents.
2. **Development status:** parts of these townships are still in land acquisition. Ask yourself whether you can wait for roads, drainage and power to come up.
3. **Total cost:** add up plot price, any location charges (corner plot, park facing, wider road), registration and stamp duty.
4. **Payment schedule:** make sure the instalments fit your cash flow. Missing payments can mean penalties or cancellation.
5. **Refund rules:** know what you get back if you're not selected or if you change your mind.
6. **Fraud:** LDA lotteries are random. Anyone promising a "fixed" allotment for money is lying.

## LDA plot vs private plot in Lucknow

Should you wait for an LDA lottery or buy a private plot? Here's an honest comparison.

**LDA plot:** clear title from the authority, planned layout, fixed rates. But you might not win the lottery, and development can take years.

**Private plot:** you choose the exact location and can often build sooner. But title verification, land use checks and approvals are entirely on you.

Plenty of buyers do both: they apply for the LDA lottery and keep looking at private options in the same area in case they don't get selected. That's a sensible approach, as long as every private deal is checked properly.
